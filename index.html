04 / One thing

Don't feel guilty.

You don't need to feel guilty because I had feelings for you. You didn't promise me anything. You didn't owe me anything. And you definitely don't need to carry responsibility for how I felt.

So please don't turn this into a “maybe I hurt him” situation. You're allowed to live your life, laugh with your friends, enjoy your days, and make your own choices.

Bas thoda sa study bhi kar lena. Boards dekh rahe hain. 📚😭

05 / Your future

Now go study, Meow.

I genuinely hope your boards go well. Don't let one difficult chapter convince you that the whole journey is difficult. One subject at a time. One chapter at a time.

  • Take proper breaks.
  • Don't panic over one bad day.
  • Focus on what you can control.
  • And please don't study at 3 AM and call it productivity. 😭
06 / Reality

About my absence.

Meri absence se shayad tumhari life mein koi huge difference nahi padega. And that's okay.

Everyone has their own people, their own routines, their own priorities. I don't need to make myself important in your life just to prove that my feelings mattered.

I just don't want to bother you anymore.

07 / Letting go

Maybe this is enough.

🌅

Some things stay beautiful

Some sunsets are beautiful precisely because they don't stay. Maybe some people are like that too. You appreciate what they meant without needing to hold onto them.

08 / If you ever ask

“Ab kya feel karta hai?”

I still care. But I've stopped trying to turn that care into something it doesn't need to become.

I don't expect anything from you. I don't want an answer. I don't want you to change your mind because you feel bad. I just want things to be normal.

09 / One last thing

There is something I should say.

I'm not angry that you rejected me. Honestly, I respect your answer. If anything feels awkward when I look back at that time, it's because I feel a little embarrassed about how I behaved.

I probably made the same topic bigger than it needed to be. I was dealing with my feelings for the first time and didn't always know how to handle them properly. That's something I can own.

10 / That's all

Just be happy.

Enjoy your life. Study well. Laugh a lot. Keep playing Hide & Seek. Keep annoying people occasionally. And don't forget to take care of yourself.

Whatever happens next, I genuinely wish good things for you. No pressure. No expectations. Just happiness.
